# Reflection on Using AI

## Three Things I Learned

1. How to provide information to AI so it can generate the output I want.
2. How to gather my information and include it in one clear prompt.
3. How to change the color of elements on a webpage.

## Adjusting Copilot's Suggestions

I changed the bright red color to a more suitable one for my page.

## Copilot as a Learning Partner

When Copilot generates everything for me, it can be difficult to understand the code and review all the changes. I can lose control over what I actually know. Using Copilot as a learning partner means taking time to understand and review the code, rather than relying on it to do all the work.

## Risks of Relying Too Much on AI

1. I may lose my ability to code.
2. I may lose the ability to solve problems by myself.
3. If I rely on quick answers to every problem, I may not build my own skills or learn how to find mistakes.
